## Critical

### 1. Fabricated testimonials and unverifiable counts

`src/pages/HomePage.jsx` published three five-star testimonials with attributed roles and cities
(*Masjid Board Member, Pittsburgh PA* · *Community Center Director, Columbus OH* · *Masjid Operations
Manager, Dearborn MI*) alongside the stats **"25+ Projects Completed"** and **"10+ Masjids Served"**.

The owner confirmed these were not real. Under **FTC 16 CFR Part 255**, publishing a fabricated
endorsement is an unfair or deceptive act — this is a legal exposure, not just a credibility one.
Supporting context: `documents/04-23-26 - PA - State Notice - AZ Audio Solutions LLC.pdf` shows the
LLC was registered 2026-04-23, roughly two months before this content shipped.

**Resolution:** testimonials section deleted outright; stats strip rebuilt from structural facts only.
A standing claims policy was added to `CLAUDE.md` to prevent recurrence.

### 2. A client's floor plans were publicly downloadable

Nothing on the site linked to them, but `public/` is served verbatim by Vercel, so these were live
and un-gated:

```
azaudios.com/masjid-real/full-final-layout.png            200, 1.9 MB
azaudios.com/masjid-real/basement-layout.png              200, 1.6 MB
azaudios.com/masjid-real/ground-floor-speaker-layout.png  200
azaudios.com/masjid-real/musalla-section.png              200
azaudios.com/masjid-real/rack-control-architecture.png    200
azaudios.com/brochure-front.png                           200, 4.2 MB
```

These match the Masjid Anisa project files in `..\masjidSoundSolutions\masjid Anisa\`. Publishing a
client's building layouts without consent is a confidentiality problem regardless of whether anyone
found them.

**Resolution:** all eight `masjid-real/` files plus the brochure and catalog PNGs deleted from `public/`.

> **Note:** deletion stops future serving, but these URLs may already be in third-party caches or
> archives. If the client relationship warrants it, that is worth checking separately.

## High

### 3. LinkedIn link returned 404

`linkedin.com/company/azaudiosolutions` → **HTTP 404**. It appeared in the footer
(`Layout.jsx`) *and* in the JSON-LD `sameAs` array (`index.html`). A broken `sameAs` entry actively
undermines the entity verification that the whole `@graph` block exists to establish.

Instagram and Facebook both resolve and were left in place.

**Resolution:** removed from both locations, with a comment noting they must be restored together.

### 4. The masjid page contradicted itself on zone count

The page stated three times that zones are not fixed at four — `"zones sized to your layout"`,
`"not limited to four"`, `"Zones configured to your layout"` — and then the Audio Signal Flow
diagram hardcoded **"4-CH AMPLIFIER"** and **"4 ZONES OUTPUT"**.

`CLAUDE.md` already claimed this had been fixed ("Amplifier shown as multi-zone (not fixed at 4)") —
the signal-flow section had been missed in that earlier pass.

**Resolution:** relabelled to `MULTI-ZONE AMPLIFIER` / `ZONE OUTPUTS`, with the four named zones kept
as a visual example and explicitly captioned *"Example layout — zones scale to your masjid"*.

### 5. Each division had two or three different names

| Nav / cards | SEO title + JSON-LD | Homepage "About" list |
|---|---|---|
| Event Rental Services | Event **Audio Solutions** | Event **Audio Solutions** |
| Commercial Audio | Commercial Audio **Solutions** | Commercial Audio **Solutions** |
| Residential Audio | Residential Audio **Solutions** | Residential Audio **Solutions** |

Inconsistent entity naming works directly against the brand-hierarchy SEO goal documented in
`CLAUDE.md` — search engines were being given three different names for the same service.

**Resolution:** one canonical name per division, applied across nav, footer, all cards, `Seo.jsx`
titles and the JSON-LD `@graph`. The footer now maps over the same `serviceLinks` array the nav uses,
so the two can no longer drift.

## Medium

### 6. Open-ended warranty claim

Process step 7 promised *"a full warranty"* with no term, scope, or exclusions stated anywhere on the
site. **Resolution:** narrowed to *"full manufacturer warranty coverage on every component we supply"* —
accurate and bounded. If a separate workmanship warranty is offered, state its term explicitly.

### 7. Implied manufacturer partnerships

The heading *"Partnering with industry leaders"* sat above nine manufacturer logos (JBL, QSC, Shure,
Sennheiser, Yamaha, TOA, DBX, RCF, Atlas IED). "Partner" implies a contractual relationship and is a
trademark-misuse risk absent a dealer agreement. **Resolution:** changed to *"Equipment we build with"*.

### 8. Three routes had no `<h1>`

`/services`, `/about` and `/contact` rendered only `SectionHeading`, which emitted `<h2>`. The 404
page had none either. **Resolution:** `SectionHeading` gained an `as` prop; all four now ship exactly
one H1. Verified across all nine routes in a browser.

### 9. `sitemap.xml` missing `/services`

Seven of eight real routes listed, despite `CLAUDE.md` asserting it covered all of them.
**Resolution:** `/services` added; `CLAUDE.md` now carries an explicit reminder to update `META` and
`sitemap.xml` together when adding a route.

### 10. Indexable soft 404s

`azaudios.com/this-page-does-not-exist` → **HTTP 200** (Vercel's SPA rewrite), and `Seo.jsx` fell back
to `META['/']`, so every typo URL got the *homepage title and description* plus a self-referencing
canonical and no `noindex`. `CLAUDE.md` claimed coverage "incl. 404" — no such entry existed.

**Resolution:** dedicated `NOT_FOUND` metadata entry and `robots: noindex, follow` for any unknown path.
Known routes explicitly get `index, follow, …`, so the tag is always correct rather than sticky.

### 11. `/services` was orphaned

Reachable only from the footer — the hero's "View Services" button targets the `#divisions` anchor —
and the page itself linked only to `/contact`, never to the four division pages it described.

**Resolution:** each card now carries an "Explore …" link to its division page alongside the quote
button. `/services` also added to the sitemap (#9).

### 13. Second stale clone of the same repo — **STILL OPEN**

`D:\work\masjidSoundSolutions\website` is a separate working copy of the **same GitHub repo**
(`boulilazied/masjid-sound-solutions`), frozen at a single commit from 2026-04-18. It contains the
retired "Masjid Sound Solutions" branding, `info@masjidsoundsolutions.com`, and a **Packages page with
public price ranges** ($2,500–$4,000 / $6,000–$12,000 / $15,000+) that no longer exists on the live site.

A push from that directory would clobber production.

**Status:** its history was archived to `D:\work\masjidSoundSolutions\website-archive-2026-08-01.bundle`
(105 KB, verified complete) — its one commit `7099440` was never pushed and existed nowhere else.
Removing the directory itself was refused by the agent sandbox; see the *Consolidation* section of
[AUDIT-FIXES.md](AUDIT-FIXES.md) for the command.

Related: the GitHub repo also carried two dead branches, `master` and `seo/az-audio-brand-authority`
— both fully merged into `main` (zero unique commits, verified). Their deletion was blocked by the
same sandbox restriction and is listed alongside.

## Known limitations not addressed

- **SPA rendering.** SEO still depends on Google executing JS. Prerendering or SSR remains the single
  biggest available win, as `CLAUDE.md` already notes. That is a build-architecture change, out of
  scope for this pass.
- **`LocalBusiness` has no street address.** Only `Pittsburgh, PA` locality. A verified Google
  Business Profile with a real address is the lever for local ranking — see `MARKETING-SEO.md`.
- **Second phone number absent from JSON-LD.** Only `+1 724 831 0196` is in the structured data;
  `+1 724 427 5661` appears in the visible footer. Intentional or not, worth a decision.
- **No submission persistence.** If Gmail SMTP fails, a quote request is lost — `api/quote.js` has no
  fallback store. Pre-existing and documented.
